## 2.2.5 Gemini edition: a lasting Gemini address, and Gemini's custom app filled in for you

See [docs/gemini.md](docs/gemini.md).

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).
- In Settings → Providers → Gemini → Add app, press **Fill in Gemini for me**. If your Gemini app
  has an old address, Gemini cannot edit it: choose **Delete** under the highlighted More options,
  then press the button again and click **Next** in Gemini yourself.
- For an address that survives restarts, use **Keep the same address** in the Tunnel step with your
  own Cloudflare tunnel.

### ✨ Added

- **Your own Cloudflare tunnel for Gemini**, next to ChatGPT's tunnel, with a lasting address. The
  token is kept in the system's secure storage.
- **Fill in Gemini for me**: the extension fills in Gemini's custom app form on your signed-in
  page and highlights the button for you to press. It never saves or deletes anything, and an
  unrecognized page gets nothing filled in.

### 🐛 Fixed

- The Add step says "Your Gemini custom app has an old address. Update it to …" instead of "not
  added" when the address changed. It keeps a fingerprint of the address, never the address.

### Limits

- Gemini cannot edit a custom app's address: an app with an old address must be deleted in Gemini
  and added again.
- Your own Cloudflare tunnel is set up in Cloudflare's dashboard (public hostname → the local
  address the step shows). Not tried against a live Cloudflare account in this release.
