## 2.2.9 Gemini edition: Gemini's model picker read correctly

See [docs/gemini.md](docs/gemini.md#models).

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).

### 🐛 Fixed

- Gemini's models are read on today's page: the read waits for the picker, which Gemini draws a
  moment after the composer.
- The picker is shut after a read. Gemini's new popover ignores Escape, so 2.2.8 could leave it
  open; your selected model is never changed.
- "Auto" is named "Auto", not "Auto Adapts to your needs".
- 2.2.8 wrongly said Gemini's page had no model picker. It has one, drawn only once the Gemini tab
  has been on screen; Settings now says to bring the tab to the front and press Refresh.

### Limits

- A Gemini tab in a covered or background window shows no picker, so no models are read there
  until the tab has been on screen.
