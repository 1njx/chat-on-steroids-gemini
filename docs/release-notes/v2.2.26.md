## 2.2.26 Settings → Gemini in your language

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).

### ✨ Changed

- Settings → Gemini follows the app's language: the setup steps, statuses, model list, error
  messages and the list of recent Gemini messages, in all 12 languages the app speaks. The names of
  Gemini's own buttons ("Custom", "Next", "Connect") stay in English, as Gemini shows them.
- Gemini error messages shown in chats are translated too.
