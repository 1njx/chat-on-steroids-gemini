## 2.2.10 Gemini edition: Gemini tabs kept awake in the background

See [docs/gemini.md](docs/gemini.md#models).

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app), then reload open Gemini tabs.

### 🐛 Fixed

- Gemini's models are read from a Gemini tab in a covered or background window. Gemini puts drawing
  its model picker off until the browser is idle, which a covered window may never be. The extension
  now keeps Gemini tabs awake, like the Focusly extension: that idle work gets a 1-second timeout,
  and the page is told it is visible and focused. Tested live on a covered tab: the picker appeared
  within 4.5 seconds, 3 times out of 3 (before: none after 27 seconds).

### Limits

- Only gemini.google.com tabs are kept awake, and only tabs opened or reloaded after the update.
