## 2.2.1 Gemini edition: Set up Gemini

Setting up Gemini is now as guided as setting up ChatGPT. See [docs/gemini.md](docs/gemini.md).

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).
- Open Settings → Providers → Gemini and follow **Set up Gemini**. Steps you already finished show as done.

### ✨ New

- **Set up Gemini.** Settings → Gemini opens with six short steps and a Ready check, laid out like
  ChatGPT's Setup: browser extension, sign in to Gemini, share a folder, an https tunnel, add the
  app in Gemini, and try it in a chat. One step shows at a time, each with one main button.
- **Steps that check themselves.** Each step turns green when the app sees it done: the extension
  connects, the Gemini page reports a signed-in account, a folder is shared, the Core address is
  https, Gemini reaches the app, and Gemini runs one of the app's tools. Ready lists every check,
  with a way back to any step that is not done yet.
- **Pictures of Gemini's pages.** Adding the app and mentioning it in a chat are shown on
  screenshots of Gemini's own pages, with numbered marks where to click (placeholder address and
  email, no account details).
- **One-click https tunnel.** The tunnel step switches from the OpenAI Secure Tunnel (which only
  serves ChatGPT) to a Cloudflare quick tunnel and connects. It says first that ChatGPT plugins
  using a tunnel ID stop working until you switch back.

### ⚠️ Known limitations

- "Add app" and "Try it" count only Gemini's calls since the app started. A quick tunnel gets a
  new address on every start, so the app has to be added again in Gemini after a restart.
- Gemini's "Save your custom app" dialog, the app in the @ menu and the "Allow once" prompt are
  described in words only.
