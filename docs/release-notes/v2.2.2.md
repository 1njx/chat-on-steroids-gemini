## 2.2.2 Gemini edition: Gemini pages open in your normal browser

A fix for opening Gemini from the app. See [docs/gemini.md](docs/gemini.md).

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).

### 🐞 Fixed

- **Gemini pages open in your normal browser.** With ChatGPT set to run "In Chat On Steroids",
  Open Gemini and Open custom apps opened gemini.google.com in the app's built-in browser, which
  has no extension and where Google may refuse sign-in. They now open in your system browser
  (or in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox when that is the chosen browser).

### ✨ New

- **Open in default browser.** Every button that opens a Gemini page has a second button beside
  it that always uses your system's default browser.
