## 2.2.24 New chats start on the model you last used

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).

### ✨ Changed

- A new chat starts on the model and thinking level you last sent with, ChatGPT or Gemini, even
  after a restart. Your default in Settings applies until you have sent something, and again right
  after you change it.

### 🐛 Fixed

- Follow-ups in a Gemini chat stay on the Gemini model you used instead of going back to Gemini's
  own default.
