## 2.2.3 Gemini edition: pick Gemini models in the chat composer

Chat with Gemini from the app's own composer. See [docs/gemini.md](docs/gemini.md).

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).
- If the composer shows no Gemini models, open gemini.google.com in your browser and press
  Refresh in Settings → Gemini once, so the app reads Gemini's model picker.

### ✨ New

- **Gemini models in the composer.** The model menu lists every model Gemini showed for your
  account, below ChatGPT's. Models that need a higher plan are shown greyed out with that reason.
  The thinking slider offers Gemini's thinking levels (for example Low, Medium, High).
- **Send to Gemini from the composer.** With a Gemini model picked, Send goes to Gemini web
  through the extension. The new Gemini chat opens in the app once Gemini answers, and later
  messages in that chat keep going to Gemini.

### ⚠️ Limits

- Gemini gets text only from the app: files, plans and edits are refused with a message.
- The question and Gemini's answer appear together once Gemini has finished.
