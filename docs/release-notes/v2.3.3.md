## 2.3.3 Long Gemini tasks no longer time out

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).

### 🐛 Fixed

- A message sent while Gemini was waiting on a tool approval never reached Gemini, and the send
  ended after 30 minutes with "Gemini did not finish answering". The app now answers that waiting
  card first (by itself with Full access, otherwise from the card in the chat), then sends your
  message.
- Long tasks with many tool calls are no longer stopped after 30 minutes. A send now ends only
  after 30 minutes without any progress, or after 4 hours in all.
