## 2.2.8 Gemini edition: Gemini's models read by themselves

See [docs/gemini.md](docs/gemini.md#models).

### 👉 After updating

- Reload the browser extension (its version moves with the app).

### 🐛 Fixed

- Gemini's models are read by themselves when a signed-in, idle Gemini page reports and no list is
  saved (or it is older than 24 hours). One read at a time, never while Gemini answers, and it never
  changes your selected model.
- A failed read backs off for 10 minutes; Settings → Gemini shows the failure beside Refresh.
- The composer's Reload queues the read even with no Gemini page open, and says "Open Gemini in
  Chrome to read its models". It never opens a page itself.
- A Gemini page without a model picker is named as such.

### Limits

- On 2026-11-22 Gemini's page showed **no model picker** on the tested account (only Chat / Task),
  so no models could be read there and none appear under Gemini in the composer. Settings → Gemini
  says so; sending still works with Gemini's own choice. If Google brings the picker back, the list
  fills by itself.
